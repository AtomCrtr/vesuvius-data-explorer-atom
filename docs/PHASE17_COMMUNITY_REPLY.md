Thanks for testing the layer order on PHerc0343P against the infrared-traced letters. We checked your reading of the air side using the team's published stacks and support masks. Before reading the layer pixels, we fixed a rule that calls the less textured end air when the two ends differ by at least a factor of 2. For each layer we measured the standard deviation in eight masked 64 × 64 patches, took their median, then averaged those medians over 13 layers at each end. [Protocol](https://github.com/AtomCrtr/vesuvius-data-explorer-atom/blob/e18fb5295d2dfb83e776e23a9e7feea7b83d6801/docs/validation/phase17-protocol.json)

On PHerc0343P, the low-numbered end (02–14) scored 3.60 and the high-numbered end (51–63) scored 21.02. The ratio of **5.83** is close to the 5.8 we saw on Frag4, where the surface orientation is known. Eight other patches also placed air at the low-numbered end (ratio 4.46). That supports your original brightness reading. On PHerc0500P2 the rule picked the same side, with a smaller margin above the threshold (2.50 against 2.00). The second set of patches gave a ratio of 4.15 there. [Texture profiles](https://github.com/AtomCrtr/vesuvius-data-explorer-atom/blob/f00fd65f8457e92ad02996990595f34f2fd4f1e9/docs/validation/phase17-orientation.json)

That helps reconcile your edit with the two Frag4 observations. On Frag4 the air-first order (`Z_in`) produced the visibly structured map. When we scored the maps against the ink labels, the papyrus-first order (`Z_out`) ranked ink better, with ROC-AUCs of 0.86 and 0.89 in the two regions. On PHerc0343P your reversed order is `Z_out` by our texture rule. Your reported rise from 0.599 to 0.936 is **consistent with our results on 5 of 6 fragments** (Frag3 is the exception, where `Z_in` did slightly better). Our new measurement on PHerc0343P establishes the orientation by that rule. The ink scores are yours. [Frag4 label evaluation](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/issues/2627#issuecomment-7053242080) · [Six-fragment summary](https://github.com/AtomCrtr/vesuvius-data-explorer-atom/blob/7ea40135ebe2f3815e3bc0bf38c1e7d19841c7df/docs/PHASE14_HELDOUT_ORDER.md)

Do you know of a render ID or manifest connecting the two?
